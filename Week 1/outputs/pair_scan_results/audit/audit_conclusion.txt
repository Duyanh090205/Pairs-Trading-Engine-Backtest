AUDIT CONCLUSION
============================================================

The final red-team audit verified all critical components of the
cointegration screening pipeline: timestamp handling, pair ordering
consistency (coint and OLS use the same A-B convention), p-value
extraction from statsmodels.coint(), BH-FDR multiple testing
correction, OU-based half-life calculation, and rejection logging.

Independent recomputation of 5 representative pairs (GOOG-GOOGL,
HD-MS, LOW-MS, XOM-CVX, BAC-JPM) confirmed exact agreement with
stored results. The BH-FDR correction at q=0.05 across 1,225 tests
requires the k-th smallest p-value to be below k/1225 * 0.05.
Only GOOG-GOOGL (p=0.000049) meets this threshold, and it is
correctly eliminated by the half-life filter as a trivial
same-company share-class pair.

Of 1225 pairs tested, 42 (3.4%) had raw p < 0.05,
consistent with the expected false positive rate under the null.

The zero-approved-pairs result is a valid empirical finding under
this methodology, not a pipeline error. The 2022 market regime
(Fed tightening, inflation, risk-off rotation) is consistent with
weakened mean-reversion dynamics across equity pairs.
